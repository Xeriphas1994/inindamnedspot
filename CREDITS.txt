Big picture
===========

All the walkthrough writers at doomwiki.org for doing the difficult, non-automatable part of the publication: actually staking out routes and describing them lucidly.

Xymph, Fredrik, Gez, Jdowland, and the other developers at doomwiki.org for releasing so much great work that this script can even be argued to be a scalability improvement.
